# Third-party data

## Trainer data (Margin/TrainerData.lua)

Data from [Efficient Games](https://efficient.games), the EG Link output for World of Warcraft: Forever
(https://github.com/JIVESCORP/eg-link-output-wowf), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed: reduced to recipe spell, required
skill and trainer fee, regenerated by `tools/gen_trainers.py`.

## Vendor prices (Margin/VendorData.lua)

Unit prices of unlimited trade goods and reagents from the same EG Link output (CC BY 4.0, vendors dataset),
plus Blizzard's Classic Era `ItemSparse` table (wago.tools) for items no opened vendor had shown yet. Changed:
reduced to item and unit price, regenerated by `tools/gen_vendor.py`.

## Recipe items (Margin/TaughtData.lua)

Which recipe item teaches which craft, from Blizzard's Classic Era `Item`, `ItemSparse`, `ItemEffect` and
`SpellEffect` tables (wago.tools), used as facts, regenerated by `tools/gen_taught.py`. World of Warcraft is a
trademark of Blizzard Entertainment.
