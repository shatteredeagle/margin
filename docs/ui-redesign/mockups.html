Margin UI Redesign

Three directions for the WoW Forever tradeskill addon. Same data, same features, three layouts. Click things: tabs, sort headers, cards, Sure?, the unpriced lookup.

Sign convention

  • Green + you earn (profit, a card that is better for you)
  • Red - you lose (route net, a worse card)
  • Plain costs stay unsigned (Cost column, shopping list, fees)

Recipe colours (the game's)

  • Orange: skill-up every craft
  • Yellow: worst case 25%, typical 75%
  • Green: worst case 5%, typical 25%
  • Grey: no skill-up
  • Blue: not learned yet (says where to learn it)
  • Make: intermediate crafted first

Money

  • g gold
  • s silver
  • c copper

Sample data is a Leatherworker at 160/225 with a locked route to 225. Prices are plausible, not live. Anything the addon does not do today carries a proposed tag.
